# Logo notes

- Idea: a flat, sticker-like jellyfish swimming up and to the right (rotated 40 degrees), faceless; the tilt, the bell and the trailing tentacles carry the mark.
- Bell: scalloped bowl built from three flat layers: cyan back shell, violet #6D4CF5 dome, pink band along the rim, plus a lime highlight arc and dot.
- Tentacles: four S-curved ribbons (pink, cyan, lime, pink) of different lengths trailing down-left, ending in round dots.
- Palette: violet #6D4CF5, pink #FF4FA3, teal #22D3C5, lime #C6F432 (olive outline #8FB51C so it reads on light), tile #1E1650.
- `public/favicon.svg`: 64x64 on a dark rounded tile so all colours keep contrast at 16 px.
- `public/logo.svg`: transparent, tight viewBox; works on #F4F3FA and #0E0F14; use at about 28 px high beside the wordmark.
- Do not recolour, add a face, or straighten the tentacles.
